# Retired and consolidated statements: fmx-respond

Read by `bin/fm-skill-compact-check.sh`, not by the agent at startup, so recording why a line went away costs no prompt budget.

The skill used to end in a "Notes" section that restated, in compressed form, rules already stated in full in the sections above.
That is the duplication `firstmate-coding-guidelines`' one-owner rule forbids: two copies of a contract drift the moment only one is edited.
Each note below is therefore consolidated into the statement that already owned the rule, not retired - every rule still binds, and the named survivor is verified to be present in the skill.

Three notes were **not** consolidated, because they carried a fact stated nowhere else, and they moved into the sections that own them:
the relay's own self-reply and per-conversation caps (now under "The three cases"), the relay being the reply-length authority (now under "Voice"), and the prohibition on editing the poll, reply, or watcher scripts (now at the end of "Procedure").
